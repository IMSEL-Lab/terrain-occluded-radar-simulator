# Authorship and source notice

Terrain-Occluded Radar Simulator was developed by Noah Young, J.C. Vaught, and Yi Wang at the University of South Carolina.

Noah Young developed the scene simulator, shared object renderer, and capture extractor. The consolidated package preserves the evaluated algorithms. Its run control, sensor adapters, evaluation runners, and package interfaces were prepared by J.C. Vaught. Yi Wang supervised the research.

The software is released under the BSD Zero Clause License (0BSD), provided in `LICENSE`.

The synthetic examples in `examples/` were constructed for this package. Dataset inputs, calibration profiles, map geometry, and evaluation results are distributed through the companion archive with source-specific attribution and terms. CANOE data is credited to the Autonomous Space Robotics Laboratory at the University of Toronto Institute for Aerospace Studies. OpenStreetMap geometry is credited to OpenStreetMap contributors.
